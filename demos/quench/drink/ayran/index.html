Ayran

Yoghurt, cold water and a pinch of salt, whisked until it froths. Lunch’s best friend.

The story

Ayran is the everyday drink of Türkiye: yoghurt thinned with cold water and seasoned with salt. It’s what you order with a döner or a plate of kebabs, because it cuts through the richness.

Versions of it are drunk across the region under other names. It is simple enough that every family has an opinion about the right amount of salt.

Ways people make it

  • With a little dried mint
  • Thinner and fizzy, sold in bottles

How it’s made

  1. 1Whisk the yoghurtLoosen plain yoghurt with a whisk until smooth.
  2. 2Add cold water and saltRoughly one part water to two of yoghurt, and a pinch of salt.
  3. 3Whisk until frothyKeep going until there’s a layer of foam on top. Serve very cold.
