Bubble tea

Milk tea with chewy tapioca pearls and a straw wide enough to catch them. Born in Taiwan in the 1980s.

the room was the fun part. here’s the reading part ↓Draft entry · facts are being checked

The story

Bubble tea, also called pearl milk tea or boba, came out of Taiwan’s tea shops in the 1980s. The “bubbles” are tapioca pearls, cooked until chewy and sweetened, sitting at the bottom of a cold milk tea.

Since then it has spread worldwide, with fruit teas, jellies and toppings. The wide straw, made for catching pearls, has stayed.

Ways people make it

  • Fruit teas with jelly instead of pearls
  • Brown sugar milk with no tea at all

How it’s made

  1. 1Cook the pearlsBoil tapioca pearls until chewy, then soak them in brown sugar syrup.
  2. 2Brew and chillStrong black tea, cooled, then mixed with milk.
  3. 3Build the cupPearls first, then ice, then milk tea. Shake it if you have a lid.
