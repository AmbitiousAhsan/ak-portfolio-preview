Irish coffee

Hot coffee, Irish whiskey and brown sugar, with a layer of lightly whipped cream floated on top. You drink the coffee through the cream.

the room was the fun part. here’s the reading part ↓Draft entry · facts are being checked

The story

The Irish coffee is usually traced to the 1940s and the flying-boat terminal at Foynes, on the Shannon estuary, where a chef named Joe Sheridan is said to have warmed up cold, delayed passengers with coffee and whiskey.

A travel writer later took the idea home to San Francisco, and from there the drink went round the world.

Ways people make it

  • With brown or demerara sugar
  • Without the whiskey, for a coffee with a cream lid

How it’s made

  1. 1Warm the glassFill it with hot water, then empty it.
  2. 2Sugar and coffeeA spoonful of brown sugar, then hot, strong coffee, stirred until the sugar dissolves.
  3. 3Add the whiskeyAbout 40 ml of Irish whiskey.
  4. 4Float the creamLightly whipped cream, poured slowly over the back of a spoon so it sits on top.
