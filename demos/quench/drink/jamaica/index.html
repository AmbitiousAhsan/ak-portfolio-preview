The story

Flor de jamaica is the Spanish name for hibiscus, and agua de jamaica is the deep red, tart agua fresca made from its dried flowers. Despite the name, it has nothing to do with the Caribbean island.

It sits next to horchata on almost every taquería counter in Mexico. One is creamy and sweet, the other bright and sour.

Ways people make it

  • With a little cinnamon or ginger
  • Frozen into paletas

How it’s made

  1. 1SteepSimmer dried hibiscus in water for a few minutes, then let it steep.
  2. 2Strain and sweetenStrain out the flowers and stir in sugar while it’s warm.
  3. 3ChillDilute with cold water, add lime if you like, and serve over ice.

Make it at home

Dried hibiscus flowers, cane sugar and a big glass jar.
