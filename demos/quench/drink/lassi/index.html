Mango lassi

Mango, yoghurt and a pinch of cardamom, blended until it’s basically dessert.

The story

Lassi is a yoghurt drink from the Punjab, the region shared by India and Pakistan, and it comes sweet or salted. Mango lassi is the version most of the world knows: yoghurt blended with ripe mango until it is thick enough to need a spoon.

Alphonso mangoes are the classic choice when they’re in season. The rest of the year, good canned pulp does the job.

Ways people make it

  • Sweet lassi, plain or with rosewater
  • Salted lassi, sometimes with roasted cumin

How it’s made

  1. 1BlendMango pulp, yoghurt, a splash of milk and a little sugar.
  2. 2SeasonA pinch of ground cardamom. Taste and adjust the sugar.
  3. 3Serve coldPour into a tall glass and finish with crushed pistachio.

Make it at home

Alphonso mango pulp, green cardamom, pistachios and two tall glasses.
