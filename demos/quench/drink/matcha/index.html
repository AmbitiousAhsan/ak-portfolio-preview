Iced matcha latte

Whisked green tea poured over cold milk, so you get two layers before you get one.

The story

Matcha is green tea ground into a fine powder, so you drink the whole leaf instead of an infusion. In Japan it’s whisked with water using a bamboo whisk, the chasen, as part of the tea ceremony.

The iced latte is a modern café drink, but it keeps the old method: whisk first, then pour.

Ways people make it

  • Hot, with steamed milk
  • Usucha, whisked with water only

How it’s made

  1. 1SiftSift the matcha into a bowl so there are no lumps.
  2. 2WhiskAdd a little warm (not boiling) water and whisk in a quick zigzag until it froths.
  3. 3LayerFill a glass with ice and milk, then pour the matcha over the top.

Make it at home

Ceremonial-grade matcha (30 g), a bamboo whisk and a wide bowl.
