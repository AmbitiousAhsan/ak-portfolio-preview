The story

Mate is an infusion of yerba mate leaves, drunk from a hollow gourd through a metal straw called a bombilla. In Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay and southern Brazil it is less a drink than a habit, carried around with a thermos of hot water.

It is made to be shared. One person, the cebador, fills the gourd and passes it round the circle, refilling it for each person in turn.

Ways people make it

  • Tereré, the cold version from Paraguay
  • Chimarrão in southern Brazil, greener and finer

How it’s made

  1. 1Fill the gourdFill it about two-thirds with yerba, tilt it, and shake so the dust settles on one side.
  2. 2Wet itA splash of cool water first, then insert the bombilla into the wet slope.
  3. 3Pour and passHot water, not boiling. Drink it all, refill, pass it on.
