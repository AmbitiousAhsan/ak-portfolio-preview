Nimbu pani

The story

Nimbu pani means lime water, and it is the summer drink of the subcontinent, sold from carts and made in every kitchen. It is sweet, salty and sour at once.

Black salt, with its faintly sulphurous edge, and a little roasted cumin are what make it taste like nimbu pani rather than lemonade.

Ways people make it

  • Sweet only, or salty only
  • Topped with soda instead of water

How it’s made

  1. 1SqueezeJuice fresh limes into a jug.
  2. 2DissolveStir in sugar, a pinch of black salt and ground roasted cumin.
  3. 3Top upAdd cold water and ice. Taste and balance.
