Fresh coconut

The story

Across the Pacific islands, the coconut palm gives food, oil, rope and roofing, and from its young green nuts, a drink. In Samoan, Tongan, Fijian and Hawaiian, the word for coconut is niu.

Drinking coconuts are picked young, before the flesh hardens, when the water inside is at its sweetest.

Ways people make it

  • Straight from the nut
  • Chilled for an hour first
  • With a squeeze of lime

How it’s made

  1. 1Pick a green oneYoung green coconuts have the most water and soft, jelly-like flesh.
  2. 2Open the topShave the top and crack it open with the opening tool, or a big knife, carefully.
  3. 3Drink, then scoopDrink the water through a straw, then split the shell and eat the soft flesh with a spoon.

Make it at home

A coconut opening tool, a flexible scoop for the soft flesh and four paper straws.
