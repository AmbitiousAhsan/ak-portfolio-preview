Pisco sour

Pisco, lime, sugar and egg white, shaken hard until the foam stands up. Both countries say it’s theirs.

the room was the fun part. here’s the reading part ↓Draft entry · facts are being checked

The story

Pisco is a grape brandy made in both Peru and Chile, and both countries claim it as their own, along with this cocktail. Each celebrates its own national pisco day.

The drink itself is simple: pisco, lime, sugar and egg white, shaken until the top turns into a thick white foam. A few drops of bitters go on top, for the look as much as the taste.

Ways people make it

  • Peruvian style, with egg white and bitters
  • Chilean style, often without the egg white

How it’s made

  1. 1Shake without icePisco, lime juice, syrup and egg white. A dry shake builds the foam.
  2. 2Shake with iceAdd ice and shake again, hard, until the tin is frosty.
  3. 3Strain and dotStrain into a coupe and finish with three drops of bitters.
