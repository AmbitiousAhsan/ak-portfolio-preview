Turkish coffee

The story

Turkish coffee is ground as fine as flour and brewed in a cezve, a small long-handled pot. It is never filtered: the grounds sink to the bottom of the cup while you wait, and the foam on top is a point of pride.

It usually comes with a glass of water, and often something sweet. UNESCO added Turkish coffee culture and tradition to its intangible heritage list in 2013.

Ways people make it

  • Sade (no sugar), az şekerli (a little) or orta (medium)
  • Served with a glass of water and often a piece of Turkish delight

How it’s made

  1. 1Measure into the cezveCold water, very finely ground coffee and sugar if you want it. Decide on the sugar now.
  2. 2Heat slowlyWarm it gently without stirring until a foam rises to the top.
  3. 3Pour with the foamPour into small cups, foam first, and let the grounds settle before you sip.
