What I take on

  • Articles and SEO content

    Long-form articles, patient and customer guides, and pages built around a keyword brief, with sources cited and claims checked.

  • Website and landing-page copy

    Service pages, homepages and campaign pages that answer the reader’s next question and make the next step obvious.

  • Editing and second looks

    Structural and line edits of other writers’ drafts, fact-checking, and change notes your team can learn from.

  • The page itself

    Copy laid out and built in HTML, CSS and JavaScript or Astro, or handed to your developers ready to drop in.

How I work inside your team

  • Your brief and style guide come first. Mine stay out of the way.
  • Your tools. Asana, Jira, SharePoint, Google Docs or whatever the team already uses.
  • Your review chain. Lead, client, subject-matter or clinical review. Questions for the expert are flagged in the draft, not after it.
  • Credited or not. Your call, project by project.
  • Healthcare handled with care. No patient data needed, no outcome claims, a source for every clinical statement.

What comes back

  • A draft in your template, with sources linked
  • A short note on the choices made and anything to confirm
  • Questions for the client or expert, listed separately
  • Formatting ready for your CMS, or the built page

How I write a piece

Brief, sources, outline, draft, expert review, final. Every stage is shown on one real article, with the turnaround: a first draft in 5 business days, two revision rounds included.
