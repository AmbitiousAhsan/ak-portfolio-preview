For clinic owners and practice managers

Marketing for clinics: what working together includes

Websites, patient content, local search, ads and reviews for medical practices, written and built by me, with the handoffs your intake and billing teams need.

What’s included

Website

Pages built around the questions patients ask before they call: care, comfort, costs and the next step. Accessible to WCAG 2.1 AA, fast on a phone, and yours.

Articles & guides

Plain-English patient content with cited sources, reviewed by your clinicians before it goes live.

Local search & reviews

Google Business Profile set up and kept current, and a review request that goes to every patient, with replies that never confirm who is a patient.

Ads

Google search and Facebook/Instagram campaigns that follow health-ad policies: no condition targeting, no implied diagnoses, no outcome promises.

Social

Useful posts, each leading to a page that answers the question it raises.

Handoffs

Inquiries arrive with a route, an owner and a next action, so intake, scheduling and billing aren’t starting from scratch.

How we’d work

  1. Listen and audit. Your current site, profiles and ads, how inquiries reach intake today, and what your team wishes patients knew before they call.
  2. Brief and plan. A written brief: audiences, voice, the questions to answer, the rules that apply and how we’ll measure progress.
  3. Write and build. Pages, articles and profiles, reviewed by you and your clinicians before anything is published.
  4. Launch and check. Accessibility and speed checks, a tracking review, and a test of every route into intake.
  5. Report and improve. A monthly report in plain English, and one or two changes worth making next.

What you own

You’re the owner of everything below. I work with user access you can remove at any time.

  • Your domain and website (code, words and images)
  • Your Google Business Profile
  • Your ad accounts and their billing
  • Your analytics and Search Console properties
  • Your social profiles

What I won’t do

  • Guarantee rankings, patient numbers or revenue
  • Write or buy reviews or testimonials
  • Promise clinical outcomes or use before-and-after claims
  • Target ads by health condition
  • Hold your accounts hostage: access is yours to remove

Privacy and tracking

  • No advertising pixels or third-party tracking on service, booking, portal or other patient-facing pages.
  • Forms that collect health information run on HIPAA-capable tools under a business associate agreement (BAA). The marketing work itself doesn’t need access to patient records.
  • Analytics are counted in aggregate: no names, conditions or visit reasons in URLs, events or reports.
  • Ad audiences use location and age, never health conditions, and never lists built from patients or site visitors.
  • Review requests go to every patient, with no gating and no incentives, following the FTC’s rules.

This is a working standard, not legal advice. Each clinic confirms its own HIPAA, Texas and payer obligations with its counsel. The healthcare case studies show these rules applied to six concepts.
