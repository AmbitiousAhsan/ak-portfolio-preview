Privacy

Only what you choose to send.

This is where clinics and content teams find my work. It doesn’t show ads, take payments or keep accounts. Here is everything it does with information, in plain words.

When you send a brief

“Send this brief” passes your brief, your name or team, and the email address you give to Web3Forms, which delivers it to my inbox at ahsankhan.direct@gmail.com. I use it only to reply to you and to talk about the work. It stays in my email; I don’t add you to a mailing list or share it with anyone.

“Email this brief” and “Copy the brief” leave the brief with you: nothing passes through the site until you send it from your own email.

When you book a call

“Book a call” opens Cal.com, which handles the booking. The details you give there go to Cal.com and to me, so I can join the call.

Visits

The site uses Google Analytics to count visits: which pages are read, roughly where visitors are, what device they use and how they arrived. It sets cookies to do this. I look only at totals, to learn which work people find useful. You can block it with your browser’s privacy settings or Google’s opt-out add-on.

It also uses Microsoft Clarity to see how pages are used: where people click and how far they scroll, including replays of visits. Clarity hides what you type into forms, and it sets cookies too. I use it to find pages that confuse people, and the same browser settings block it.

Google Tag Manager loads these tools on each page. It doesn’t collect anything itself. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, also counts visits in total.

What stays in your browser

Some choices are remembered on your own device and never sent anywhere: daylight or night mode, reading settings on the Linden & Hale site, and the bag on the Quench site. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

The sample sites

Kitewell, Reframe Orthopedics, Cedarline Care, Solenne Skin, Juniper Women’s Health, Goodkind Dental, Linden & Hale, Dr. Mara, Quench and FORME Studio are working websites for brands that don’t exist. Their forms, bags and bookings are part of the demonstration: nothing you enter in them is sent to me or anyone else.

Deleting what you sent

Write to ahsankhan.direct@gmail.com and I’ll delete your brief and our emails, and tell you when it’s done.
