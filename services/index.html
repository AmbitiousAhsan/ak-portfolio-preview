What runs in the background

The busywork runs itself.
I do the thinking.

Clinics hire agencies because there’s too much to keep track of. I set up a system for each clinic that tracks it, so routine work never waits on anyone and my time goes into the parts that need judgment.

  1. 01

    Every inquiry reaches the right person

    Messages from your website and Google profile are sorted by what they’re about (new patient, referral, billing question) and go to the right person on your team, with the next step attached. Sorted by type, never by condition.

  2. 02

    Every patient gets the same review request

    Asked the same way after every visit, with no gating and no incentives. New reviews come to me for a reply that never confirms who is a patient.

  3. 03

    A content calendar that keeps itself

    Articles, posts and profile updates are planned a month ahead. Drafts reach you ready to approve, not as another task on your list.

  4. 04

    A monthly report in plain English

    Inquiries, calls, reviews and where you show up on Google Maps, gathered automatically. I write what changed and the one or two things worth doing next.

  5. 05

    A workspace that is only yours

    Your brief, voice rules, approvals and every file, kept in one place I set up for your clinic. Nothing depends on my memory or my inbox.

Automations do the busywork. I review every word. You approve before anything goes live. The marketing never needs your patient records. What working together includes, with a sample monthly report.

Questions

Before we
get started.

How do I hire you?+

Book a call or send a brief. I work as an independent contractor: on a project, an ongoing contract or a full-time contract, inside your tools and review process. I do the writing, the design and the build myself, with automations for the routine work. If something needs a specialist, like photography or legal review, I say so up front.

What does it cost?+

I don’t publish prices, because one patient article and a full clinic website are very different jobs. On the free call I ask what you need and what you can spend. You get a written price before any work starts, and it doesn’t change unless the scope does.

How fast is a patient article?+

The first draft arrives within 5 business days of an agreed brief. Two rounds of revisions are included.

Do you need access to patient records?+

No. The marketing never needs them. Forms that collect health information run on HIPAA-capable tools under a business associate agreement, and reports count in aggregate.

What do you need from me to start?+

A rough idea is enough. We’ll turn it into a brief together: the reader, the purpose, the deliverables and the deadline.

Can you build the website as well as write it?+

Yes, when the project calls for it. Frontend implementation can be part of a website content project when it’s scoped in.

Do you take full-time contracts?+

Yes. I’m available for full-time contracts, ongoing support and focused projects.

Do you use AI?+

Where it helps the work. I review the output, check the sources and make the editorial decisions. That responsibility stays with me.

How do reviews work?+

We agree on review points at the start. Feedback is carried into the copy, and important changes come with the reasoning behind them.
