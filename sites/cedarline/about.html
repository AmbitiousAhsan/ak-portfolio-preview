About the concept

An original practice concept. A working website.

A fictional primary + urgent care practice, built to show how a clinic website can route people to the right visit and support what happens after it.

The brief

Busy adults comparing a new primary care practice with a visit for a more immediate, non-emergency concern. A single “book now” button hides several different care needs.

The design idea

Cedarline is designed like civic wayfinding: every kind of visit is a lettered door on one of two lines, Today and Ongoing, and both lines end at the same regular care team. The “Which door?” chooser, the hours board and the map use that one sign system. Switch to the clinic owner view in the top bar to see what each part does for the practice.

The reference

ZoomCare services informed the research direction: separate visit types and show the practical route into care. The reference organisation did not commission, endorse or take part in this project.

What is built

Care pages, three guides, four social previews, a visit planner with no fields, an hours board and a follow-up route. Brand identity, pictograms, copy and generated imagery were made for this portfolio.

What is not claimed

No operating clinic, real providers, patient research, clinical review, system integration, live campaign or measured result. Locations, hours and scenes are fictional. A real launch would need verified operational content and professional review.

Typefaces: Overpass, Overpass Mono and Public Sans, self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License (Overpass, Overpass Mono, Public Sans).

Voice

  1. Answer the question she arrived with first.Do: Walk-ins at Market Street (sample hours). Call first if you can.Don’t: Welcome to Cedarline, where your health is our priority.
  2. Name the door, not the department.Do: Door A: urgent care, for something new that can’t wait.Don’t: Our acute episodic care service line.
  3. Say what happens next, never how fast.Do: Before you leave, you’ll know who calls you about the result.Don’t: Be seen in 15 minutes or less!

Persona

Dana Ruiz, 38. Works at a school, two kids, moved last year and hasn’t found a regular doctor yet. Tonight her son twisted his ankle at practice.

She wants

  • To be seen today without an emergency room bill
  • To leave with a family doctor, not just a receipt

She worries about

  • Long waits she can’t plan around
  • A bill she didn’t see coming
  • Being rushed
  • Repeating her history to a new face every time

The homepage follows her evening: what brings you in, the two doors, the regular team, the place and its hours, the bill, then what to bring.
