What happens if I just turn up?

At Market Street, you can. Here’s how a walk-in visit usually goes:

  1. The front desk asks what happened and checks it isn’t an emergency.
  2. You wait to be seen. We’ll tell you roughly how long if we can.
  3. A clinician assesses, explains and treats what can be treated today.
  4. The visit is written up for your regular care team, so nobody starts again.

Chest pain, trouble breathing or sudden weakness: call 911 instead.
