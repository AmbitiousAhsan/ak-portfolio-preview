Cedarline · guide 03DTests & follow-up

Ten things for the walk home

Before you leave the room

  1. 1Who will explain my results?
  2. 2How will I hear, and roughly when?
  3. 3Is there a referral I need to arrange myself?
  4. 4Do I need a follow-up visit?

At home

  1. 5Write down what the clinician said while it’s fresh
  2. 6Note any test dates and who ordered them
  3. 7Keep the visit summary with your records

Get back in touch if

  1. 8The time you were given passes without news
  2. 9The plan isn’t working, or you have a new question
  3. 10Something gets worse: call the team, or 911 in an emergency

What to say when you call

“I had a test on [date], ordered by [clinician]. I’m calling to ask who will explain the result.”
