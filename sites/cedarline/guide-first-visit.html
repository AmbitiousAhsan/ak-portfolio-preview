Eleven things to sort before your first visit

Bring

  1. 1Your medication and supplement list, with doses
  2. 2Names of specialists you see
  3. 3The pharmacy you use
  4. 4Any vaccination records you have
  5. 5Records or recent results from your last practice

Write down

  1. 6The reason for your visit, in your own words
  2. 7The two or three questions that matter most to you

Ask before you leave

  1. 8How do I message the team between visits?
  2. 9How are refills handled?
  3. 10Who explains a result, and how soon?
  4. 11Who follows up on tests and referrals?
