Your regular team

The same faces, visit after visit.

A regular care team means you tell your story once. Three roles look after you, and each one has a question worth asking.

  • Primary care teamYour regular care: the bigger picture, preventive conversations and follow-up after a visit.
  • Urgent care teamA new problem on the day that isn’t an emergency: an assessment, an explanation and the next step.
  • Visit coordinatorGets your request to the right door, sorts out the practical details and tells you who has the next step. Clinical questions go to a clinician.

Questions

What people ask before they come in.

How follow-up works
QDo I need an appointment for urgent care?

No. Market Street takes walk-ins for urgent care during its sample hours. If you can, call first so the team can check they can see your concern today.

QIs urgent care billed like the emergency room?

No, they’re billed differently, and plans treat them differently too. Ask your plan how it covers each, and check that the building you’re walking into is urgent care.

QCan my kids be seen here?

Sports and school physicals are booked through Door C, and a sprain or a fever can go to Door A. Ask when you call which ages each door sees.

QWill I have to repeat my whole history?

Not every time. Your regular team keeps your record, and urgent care notes go to them. Bring your medication list to the first visit and it’s there from then on.

QDo you take my insurance?

Check your plan rather than a list on a website: ask whether this clinician, at this location, is in network, and whether you need a referral. The costs page lists the questions.

QWhat if it’s an emergency?

Call 911, or go to the nearest emergency room. Don’t wait for an urgent care visit. For a mental health crisis, call or text 988.
