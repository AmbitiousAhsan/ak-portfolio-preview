How follow-up works

From the first call to the bill, step by step.

Who handles each step after you get in touch, and what to do if something stalls.

01

Clinic owner note 1 This is the operating model behind the site, written for patients. The demo shows the handoffs; it doesn’t connect to an EHR, scheduling, eligibility, claims or payment system, and a real build would need those confirmed, reviewed and tested.

  1. Step 1 · Get in touch

    You call, book or walk in

    Tell us which door you think you need. If you’re not sure, the visit coordinator will help you choose.

  2. Step 2 · Right door

    The visit coordinator checks the fit

    They confirm the service, the location and a real time, and tell you what to bring.

  3. Step 3 · Registration

    Your details and coverage are checked

    New patients register first. Coverage questions get answered here, before the visit, where possible.

  4. Step 4 · Your visit

    A clinician sees you and records the visit

    Decisions about your care are the clinician’s, made with you. The notes go into your record.

  5. Step 5 · Next step

    Results and referrals get a named owner

    Before you leave, you know who will contact you about each result or referral, and how.

  6. Step 6 · The bill

    Billing comes after review

    The visit is billed once it has been documented and reviewed. If a statement doesn’t make sense, the billing team explains it.

02

Clinic owner note 2 Steps 3 to 6 are where clinical information lives. On a real build it stays in the approved intake and clinical systems; nothing from the website’s chooser or planner reaches a marketing tool.

If something stalls

  • No call back about booking: call the visit coordinator.
  • No news about a result by the time you were given: call the care team.
  • A referral you haven’t heard about: ask who sent it and where.
  • A statement you don’t understand: ask the billing team to go through it.

What stays private

  • The website never asks about symptoms or your health.
  • Health details are given to the clinic directly, by phone or at the visit.
  • Please don’t send health information through social media.
