Overview

Cedarline is a primary and urgent care practice built so that today’s visit reaches the regular team. Urgent care takes walk-ins at Market Street; Cedar Park is booked visits only. Every urgent care note is routed to the patient’s primary care team, and every result has a named owner who explains it.

Why Cedar Park has no walk-ins.

Two buildings doing the same job split the queue. Market Street takes every walk-in; Cedar Park keeps booked visits calm and on time. The website says so on every page, so nobody arrives at the wrong door.

How a visit is handed off

  1. Visit reason and preferred setting
  2. Scheduling team confirms scope and availability
  3. Approved registration and benefits checks
  4. Clinician documents care in the clinical record
  5. Results, referrals and patient questions assigned
  6. Billing review, claim and patient statement
