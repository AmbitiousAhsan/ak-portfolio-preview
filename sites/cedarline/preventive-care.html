What a check-up covers, and what may not be part of it

Usually part of the check-up

  • A review of your history, routines and priorities
  • Which screenings or vaccinations may be due
  • A plan for what to do before the next check-up

May be billed separately

  • A new problem you’d like looked at during the same visit
  • Tests that go to an outside lab
  • A follow-up visit about something the check-up finds
Why the check-up can turn into two bills
01

Clinic owner note 1 This is the billing conversation moved to before the visit: what a wellness visit includes, and when a new problem becomes a separate charge. It’s the statement your billing team explains most after check-ups.

Records worth finding

  • Past screening results, or the dates you had them
  • Vaccination records, or photos of them
  • Any family history you know

How the visit runs

  1. 1

    Go through your history, routines and what matters to you.

  2. 2

    Talk through screening or vaccination questions with the clinician.

  3. 3

    Agree what this visit includes, and what would need a separate one.

02

Clinic owner note 2 Asking for records up front gives the clinician history before the visit rather than during it.

People ask

QIs every part of a wellness visit covered?

Don’t assume so. Ask the practice and your plan what this visit includes, and whether anything you add on the day is billed separately.
