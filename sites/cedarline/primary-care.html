Clinic owner note 2 Splitting new and returning patients tells intake which forms and records requests to send before the visit, instead of at the desk.

Between visits

Messages

Ask how to reach the team between visits, and what kind of question suits a message rather than a visit.

Refills

Ask how refills are requested and how much notice the team needs before you run out.

Results

Ask who explains a result, and how you’ll hear about it.

03

Clinic owner note 3 Messages, refills and results are what patients call about between visits. Saying how each works here is the explanation the front desk would otherwise give on the phone.

People ask

QCan I see the same care team for follow-ups?

Continuity is worth asking about before you choose a practice. A real clinic can tell you which clinicians are taking new patients and how follow-up visits are booked.
