Demo privacy

No patient information belongs in this demo.

Everything here uses preset choices. There are no health questions, uploads, payments or messages.

Local interactions

The “Which door?” chooser, the planner, the cost list, the hours board and the view switch work in this page’s memory only. Follow, save, filters and storyboards change only the preview. Refreshing clears them. A guide download is made by your browser.

Nothing is submitted

The chooser and planner don’t send a request, book an appointment, create a record or contact a practice. Don’t rely on them to get care.

Hosting and portfolio

The hosting platform may process ordinary request data to serve the site. This build has no analytics tags. See the portfolio privacy page for the wider website.

Real-world requirements

A real practice would need approved collection, consent, security, retention and access processes. No healthcare privacy certification is claimed for this concept.
