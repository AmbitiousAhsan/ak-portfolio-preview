C

Room for the check-up

Preventive visits

A check-up that looks at your history, your routines and what’s due, before anything is wrong.

Is this your door? You’re due a wellness visit, want to talk about screening or vaccinations, or haven’t had a check-up in a while.

Records worth finding

  • Past screening results, or the dates you had them
  • Vaccination records, or photos of them
  • Any family history you know

D

After the appointment

Tests & follow-up

Know who is responsible for the next answer, not just where the sample goes.

Is this your door? A test has been ordered or done, and you want to know where it happens, what it costs and who tells you the result.

Before the test

  • Preparation instructions, such as fasting, in writing
  • Where the test happens, and whether you need to book
  • Whether the lab is in network for your plan
