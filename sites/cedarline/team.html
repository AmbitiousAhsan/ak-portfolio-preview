Who you’ll meet

Know who handles the next question.

Three roles, and what to ask each one.

Role 01

Primary care team

Your regular care: the bigger picture, preventive conversations and follow-up after a visit.

Ask this before you choose

Who will I see, what are their qualifications, and who provides follow-up?

Role 02

Urgent care team

A new problem on the day that isn’t an emergency: an assessment, an explanation and the next step.

Ask this before you choose

How does this team share visit notes with my regular care team?

Role 03

Visit coordinator

Gets your request to the right door, sorts out the practical details and tells you who has the next step. Clinical questions go to a clinician.

Ask this before you choose

How will I know the next step has been arranged, and who do I call if it hasn’t?

Room to say what matters

A good visit starts with listening.

  1. 1

    Tell the team what would help.

    Ask about access, an interpreter, bringing someone with you or an explanation before each step.

  2. 2

    Ask for a clear explanation.

    Talk through options, consent, costs and what needs deciding now or later.

  3. 3

    Know how to raise a concern.

    Ask who handles questions about care, communication and billing.
