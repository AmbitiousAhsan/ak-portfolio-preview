Where a result goes, and who owns it

  1. OrderedThe clinician who ordered it
  2. Sample takenThe testing service
  3. ProcessedThe lab, which may bill separately
  4. ReviewedThe ordering clinician
  5. You’re toldThe care team, by the route you agreed
01

Clinic owner note 1 Every result needs an owner. Showing who explains it is the answer to the question behind most “any news?” calls, and the lab step warns about the separate bill before it arrives.

Before the test

  • Preparation instructions, such as fasting, in writing
  • Where the test happens, and whether you need to book
  • Whether the lab is in network for your plan

If you hear nothing

Ask at the test when you should expect to hear. If that time passes, call the care team: a result isn’t “fine” until someone has told you so.

The after-visit checklist
02

Clinic owner note 2 A clear “if you hear nothing” rule gives patients a point at which to call, instead of calling every day or not at all.

People ask

QWill a result appear before someone explains it?

It can, depending on the practice’s system. Ask how its results process works and who to contact if you see a result before anyone has explained it.
