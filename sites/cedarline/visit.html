    How follow-up works

    01

    Clinic owner note 1 No fields and no personal data: the planner captures intent (door, place, time preference, one note) and shows what intake would receive. Clinical detail stays out of the website.

    02

    Clinic owner note 2 When the chooser, a care page or a guide sends the visit type in the link (?service=), the planner skips that question and says where the patient came from, so the handoff is shorter and the source is visible.

    03

    Clinic owner note 3 The last screen is the handoff: who reads the request, what they check, what the patient should bring. In a real build this is the booking confirmation and the intake record.
