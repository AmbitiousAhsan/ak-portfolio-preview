What happens at a cleaning, step by step

If it’s been a while, a cleaning can feel like the unknown. Here’s what usually happens, in order:

  1. A look at your teeth and gums, with the hygienist saying what they see.
  2. Scaling, to remove hardened tartar. It can sound scratchy. That’s normal.
  3. Polishing, with a slightly gritty paste.
  4. Flossing, plus a tip or two if you want them.

If anything’s uncomfortable, raise your hand. That’s what the stop signal is for.
