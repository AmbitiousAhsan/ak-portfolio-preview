A sample first visit back

Your first hour, roughly.

Times are a sample. A real practice will tell you how long its first visit runs.

  1. 0 min

    Check in

    Mention it’s been a while. If you made a comfort card, hand it over now.

  2. 5 min

    Talk, sitting up

    Your worries, your history and what you’d like from today. You can stop here if that’s enough for one visit.

  3. 15 min

    A look, with narration

    The dentist checks teeth and gums and says what they’re doing as they go. If X-rays are suggested, ask why today.

    How often do you need X-rays? →
  4. 35 min

    Cleaning, maybe

    After a long gap, a full cleaning sometimes needs its own visit. Ask what today includes before it starts.

  5. 50 min

    The plan, on paper

    What needs doing, what can wait and the estimate, written down before anything else is booked.

3

For the clinic. The words patients hear in the chair, decoded, so your hygienist isn’t explaining “threes and fours” for the first time mid-exam.

Words you might hear

Chair-side words, decoded.

Plaque and tartar
Plaque is the soft film a toothbrush removes. Tartar is plaque that has hardened, and only a dental cleaning removes it.
“Threes and fours”
Numbers read out during a gum check. Each one is the depth, in millimetres, of the small gap between gum and tooth. Ask what yours mean.
Bitewings
Small X-rays of the back teeth that show the sides, where cavities between teeth hide.
“Let’s watch that”
A spot the dentist wants to check again next time instead of treating now. Ask when “next time” is.
