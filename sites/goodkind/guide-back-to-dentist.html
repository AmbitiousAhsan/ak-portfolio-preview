Coming back after a while?

Prepare for the conversation without having to explain everything perfectly.

1

For the clinic. This guide is the offer behind the social posts. It’s ungated (no email, no form), and the call line gives patients the words for their first phone call.

Goodkind pocket guide 01

Before you call

  1. Tell the practice you are returning after a gap.
  2. Ask how long the first visit usually takes.
  3. Ask whether the first visit can be a talk only.
  4. Find your old dentist’s name, if you remember it, so records can be requested.

At the first visit

  1. Name any concerns or previous difficult experiences.
  2. Ask how the first assessment is structured.
  3. Agree a stop signal before anything starts.
  4. Ask whether X-rays are suggested, and why today.

Before you agree to anything

  1. Request a clear plan before agreeing to treatment.
  2. Ask which parts are urgent and which can wait.
  3. Ask for the estimate in writing, stage by stage.

What to say when you call

“Hi, it’s been a few years since I saw a dentist and I’m a bit nervous. Can my first visit be a check-up and a talk?”
