Pocket guide 02 · Written-estimate checklist

Before you say yes to a treatment plan.

Make the total plan easier to understand, including questions about financing.

1

For the clinic. This guide is the offer behind the social posts. It’s ungated (no email, no form), and the call line gives patients the words for their first phone call.

Goodkind pocket guide 02

On the written plan

  1. Ask which services and stages are included.
  2. Check that each service names the tooth or area.
  3. Ask which parts are time-sensitive.
  4. Ask what could change the estimate once care starts.

Ask your dental plan

  1. Separate the practice estimate from the insurer’s decision.
  2. Ask how much of your yearly maximum is left.
  3. Ask about waiting periods and how often cleanings and X-rays are covered.
  4. Ask whether a pre-treatment estimate is available.

Before you sign

  1. If financing is offered, ask about interest, fees and repayment terms.
  2. Keep a copy of the plan.
  3. Know who answers billing questions.

What to say when you call

“I’ve been given a treatment plan and I’d like to go through the estimate before I book. Who can I talk to?”
