Pocket guide 03 · Cosmetic consultation planner

Bring a smile goal. Take home better questions.

Explore the decision beyond a before-and-after photo.

1

For the clinic. This guide is the offer behind the social posts. It’s ungated (no email, no form), and the call line gives patients the words for their first phone call.

Goodkind pocket guide 03

Your goal

  1. Write down what you would like to change, in your own words.
  2. Note what you like about your smile now.
  3. Decide what budget questions you want answered.

In the consultation

  1. Ask what is suitable for your oral health.
  2. Discuss the limits and alternatives.
  3. Ask what a realistic result looks like for you.
  4. Understand maintenance and future replacement needs.
  5. Ask whether any health treatment should come first.

Before you decide

  1. Review a written estimate without a countdown offer.
  2. Ask what happens if you change your mind partway.
  3. Take the plan home before you agree.

What to say when you call

“I’d like a cosmetic consultation, just to talk through options and cost. No treatment on the day.”
