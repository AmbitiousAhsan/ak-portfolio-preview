Your first visit

Your first visit, in three steps.

Nothing happens to your teeth until you’ve talked, and nothing is booked until you’ve seen the plan.

  1. Step 1

    Talk, sitting up

    Your worries, your history and what you’d like from today, face to face before any exam. That can be the whole first visit.

  2. Step 2

    A gentle look, narrated

    The dentist checks your teeth and gums and says what they’re doing as they go. Raise your hand and they stop.

  3. Step 3

    The plan, on paper

    What needs doing, what can wait and the estimate, in writing, before anything else is booked.

    For the clinic. The three questions returning patients ask most on the first call, answered before they dial.

    Asked a lot

    The questions people bring back with them.

    Will I be judged for leaving it so long?

    You can say so up front, or put “no lecture” on your comfort card. The first visit starts from today.

    Can my first visit just be a talk?

    Ask for that when you call. A talk-only first visit is a reasonable request, and the front desk can tell you what the practice offers.

    What if it hurts, or I need a break?

    Agree a stop signal before anything starts and ask what each step will feel like. Your dentist decides what numbing is suitable and can explain the options first.

    What if it has been years since my last visit?

    Say so when you contact a real practice and ask how a first assessment works. You can discuss comfort, cost and the order of care before agreeing to a plan.

    Do I have to agree to the whole plan at once?

    Ask the dentist which parts are time-sensitive, how options compare and whether care can be staged. The answer depends on an individual assessment.

    Will I know the cost before anything happens?

    Ask for a written plan with the practice’s estimate before you agree to treatment. Your dental plan decides what it pays, so check with them too.

    Can I say no to part of a plan?

    Yes. A plan is a proposal. You can ask what can wait, choose a different option or take it home to think.
