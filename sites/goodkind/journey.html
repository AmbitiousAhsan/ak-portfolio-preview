For practice teams · marketing → care → billing

A clearer route from the first click to follow-up.

The proposed operational model behind the website. The demo shows the handoffs; it does not connect to clinical, insurance or payment systems.

1

For the clinic. This page is for you, not patients: the proposed route from a post to the chair to the bill, and what a real integration would need.

  1. Marketing01

    Visit purpose and comfort preference

    The social post and visit page carry the visit purpose and comfort preferences into the sample planner. No symptom or patient detail enters a marketing tool.

  2. Marketing02

    Patient coordinator confirms an appropriate visit

    A real practice must confirm the right visit, the responsible team and actual availability.

  3. Care03

    Approved records and benefit questions

    Clinical information and benefit questions belong in an approved intake or clinical system with appropriate access, consent and retention controls.

  4. Care04

    Dental assessment and agreed treatment options

    An authorized dentist owns assessment, treatment decisions and documentation. A website does not create clinical decisions.

  5. Billing05

    Written plan, consent and staged appointments

    The written plan names stages, estimates and who owns each appointment. Consent is recorded through the practice’s approved process.

  6. Billing06

    Claim review, patient billing and agreed recall route

    Billing depends on documented services, plan rules and review. No claim acceptance, payment or collections result is implied.

What the website demonstrates

  • Visit-specific routes from social posts and pocket guides
  • A comfort card made in the browser, never sent
  • A sample visit plan that picks up the card and the visit from the link
  • Separate cost, care and administrative questions

What real integration would require

  • Confirmed systems, vendor access and data ownership
  • Clinical, privacy and operational review
  • Field mapping, authorization and failure handling
  • Testing, audit trails and accountable support owners

No EMR, EHR, practice-management, scheduling, eligibility, claims or payment integration has been completed. This is a proposed architecture for discussion, not evidence of a healthcare implementation.
