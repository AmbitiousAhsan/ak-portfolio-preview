Overview

Goodkind is a neighborhood dental practice built around people who have put off the dentist. A first visit can be a conversation only. Comfort preferences travel with the patient as care details. Every treatment plan is written down, with the estimate and the dental plan’s decision kept apart, before anything is booked.

Talk-first dentistry

3w · Public

Why our hygienists agree a stop signal before anything starts.

A raised hand costs ten seconds. A patient who doesn’t trust that they can pause costs a missed recall. We agree the signal sitting up, before the chair tips back, and we stop every time it’s used.

How a first visit back runs

How a visit is handed off

  1. Visit purpose and comfort preference
  2. Patient coordinator confirms an appropriate visit
  3. Approved records and benefit questions
  4. Dental assessment and agreed treatment options
  5. Written plan, consent and staged appointments
  6. Claim review, patient billing and agreed recall route
