Your arrival

From the entrance to the talk room.

Ask about waiting-area needs, interpretation, mobility support, a quieter arrival or a support person before you book.

  1. Step 1

    Find the entrance

    Check the entrance before you set off, and ask about parking and step-free access.

  2. Step 2

    Say hello at the desk

    Mention it’s your first visit, or your first in a while. Show your comfort card if you made one.

  3. Step 3

    Settle in

    Ask about a quieter place to wait, or whether you can wait outside until you’re called.

  4. Step 4

    Talk before the chair

    The first conversation happens sitting up, in a consultation room, before any exam.
