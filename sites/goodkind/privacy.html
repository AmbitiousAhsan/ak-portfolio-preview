Demo privacy

No patient information belongs in this demo.

Use the preset choices to explore the experience. There are no health-history fields, uploads, payments or clinical messages.

Local interactions

The comfort menu, visit planner, view switch, follow, save and storyboard controls use the current page’s memory and its link only. Refreshing clears them. The comfort card image and the pocket-guide downloads are created by your browser; nothing is uploaded.

No clinical submission

The planner does not submit a request, reserve an appointment, create a patient record or contact a practice. The Call and Book buttons do not place a call or book a visit. Do not rely on this site to obtain care.

Hosting and portfolio

The hosting platform may process ordinary request data to serve the site. The private review build contains no portfolio analytics tags. See the portfolio privacy page for the wider website.

Real-world requirements

A real practice would need approved collection, consent, security, retention, access and communication processes. No healthcare privacy compliance certification is claimed for this concept.
