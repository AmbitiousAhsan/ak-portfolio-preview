What happens at a first gynecology visit?

At Juniper, a first visit starts as a conversation.

  1. You say what you’d like to talk about.
  2. If the clinician suggests an examination, they explain it first and ask before starting.
  3. You can bring someone, or ask for a chaperone.

Put what you want on a conversation card before you come. Nothing is sent; you bring it with you.
