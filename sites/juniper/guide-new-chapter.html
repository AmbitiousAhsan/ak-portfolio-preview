Menopause conversation guide

Make room for the changes you’re noticing.

Prepare for a focused discussion about daily life and your priorities.

Juniper · guide three

Eleven questions for a perimenopause or menopause visit

Two weeks before

  1. Note your period dates for the last few months.
  2. Note sleep, hot flashes or night sweats, and when they happen.
  3. Note mood, focus or energy changes that affect your days.
  4. List your medicines and supplements.

In the conversation

  1. Name the change that affects you most.
  2. Ask which options suit your history, including non-hormonal ones.
  3. Ask about the benefits, limits and risks of each.
  4. Ask how long before you’d know if something is helping.

After

  1. Agree when you’ll review the plan together.
  2. Ask what change should prompt a call sooner.
  3. Any bleeding after menopause: tell a clinician promptly.
