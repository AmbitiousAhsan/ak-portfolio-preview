Eleven questions before you choose a team

Choosing a team

  1. Which clinicians might I see: obstetricians, midwives, or both?
  2. Where would I give birth, and which hospital does the team work with?
  3. How many appointments are typical, and where do they happen?
  4. Can I meet more than one clinician during my care?

Starting or moving care

  1. When is the first appointment usually booked?
  2. How do you request my records if I’m transferring?
  3. Which scans and tests are usually offered, and which are optional?
  4. Who explains what prenatal care and delivery will cost?

Between visits

  1. Who answers urgent questions, day and night?
  2. What should make me call straight away?
  3. How do I reach the team after hours?
