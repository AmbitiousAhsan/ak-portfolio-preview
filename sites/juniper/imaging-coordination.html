Imaging coordination. Know what is being arranged.

Practical guidance for discussing an ordered ultrasound or other imaging visit.

“A scan has been ordered. What happens now?”

Imaging has been recommended and you want to understand where, when and how it connects to your care.

From order to result

  1. Order

    Your clinician

    Orders the scan and explains why.

  2. Booking

    Imaging coordinator

    Finds a slot and sends preparation instructions.

  3. The scan

    Imaging center

    Carries out the scan. Ask about comfort and who can come in.

  4. Reading

    Radiologist

    Reviews the images and writes a report.

  5. Your result

    Your clinician

    Talks you through it and agrees what happens next.

01

For the clinic · note 1“Who has my result?” is a common call after a scan. The tracker names an owner for each step, so patients know who to ask.

On the day

  • The ordering team’s instructions
  • Questions about cost and location
  • Any access or support preferences
  • Ask about the appointment process and comfort preferences.

About the bill. The imaging center may bill you for the scan, and a radiologist may bill separately for reading it. Cost questions for scans

02

For the clinic · note 2Saying up front that the imaging center bills separately saves the billing team explaining two bills afterwards.

Questions you might have

You might askCan I book any scan directly?

Something to knowRequirements differ by service. Confirm whether an order is needed and follow the instructions from the responsible care team.

You might askDoes the scan happen where my visit was?

Something to knowNot always. Confirm the location and its access details with the team that arranged it.

You might askWho tells me the result?

Something to knowAsk who reviews it, how you will hear and what to do if you haven’t heard by the time they said.
