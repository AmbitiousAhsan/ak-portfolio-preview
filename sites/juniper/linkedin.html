Overview

Juniper is a women’s health practice built around the conversation a patient wants to have, not an assumed life plan. Patients name the topic and their comfort needs on a conversation card; one coordinator routes gynecology, pregnancy and menopause requests; and every next step, from a scan to a bill question, has a named owner.

Why we don’t build ad audiences from care interests.

A visit to a menopause page or a pregnancy guide is sensitive information. In this concept, campaigns are measured with approved, aggregate numbers and voluntary usability sessions, never by retargeting someone because of the care they read about.

The first visit starts in the armchairs.
