Where we meet.

Two settings, each with private consultation rooms. Choose whichever is easier to reach.

01

For the clinic · note 1Two sample settings show what a location page should answer before someone travels: entrance, access, where to wait. They are design scenarios, not operating clinics.

Sample details

Juniper House

Address
1 Garden Row, Northside (sample address)
Hours
Mon–Thu 8:00–6:00Fri 8:00–4:00Sat 9:00–12:00 (sample hours)
Parking
Small lot behind the building, plus street parking
Transit
Bus stop at the corner, about a 3-minute walk
Step-free
Step-free side entrance and a ground-floor consultation room. Confirm before you travel.

Sample details

Eastbank Rooms

Address
40 River Street, Eastbank (sample address)
Hours
Mon–Fri 9:00–5:30Sat ClosedSun Closed (sample hours)
Parking
No on-site parking; a public garage is a block away
Transit
Eastbank tram stop, about a 2-minute walk
Step-free
Lift to the first-floor rooms. Confirm before you travel.
02

For the clinic · note 2The location card answers what people phone to ask: where, when, parking, transit and step-free access. Every value is labelled sample until verified.

Things you can ask before you arrive

  • Which entrance to use, and whether it is step-free
  • Whether your kind of visit happens at this setting
  • Where to wait, and whether a quieter space is possible
  • Whether a support person or interpreter can join you
