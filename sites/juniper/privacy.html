Juniper/Demo privacy

Demo privacy

No patient information belongs in this demo.

Every interaction uses preset buttons. There are no text fields, health-history questions, uploads, payments or clinical messages.

The conversation card stays on your device

The card is assembled in the current page’s memory from the buttons you press. Topics you pick on the homepage travel to the card page in the link itself. Printing uses your browser’s print dialog. Downloading creates a plain-text file in your browser. Nothing is sent, stored or shared, and refreshing the page clears it.

Other local interactions

The Patient view and Clinic owner view switch is carried in the link (?view=clinic), not stored. Menus, follow, save, slide and storyboard controls change only the page you’re on while it is open. A guide download is created by your browser.

No clinical submission

The card does not submit a request, reserve an appointment, create a patient record or contact a practice. Do not rely on it to obtain care.

Hosting and portfolio

The hosting platform may process ordinary request data to serve the site. The site makes no third-party requests and contains no analytics tags. See the portfolio privacy page for the wider website.

Real-world requirements

A real practice would need approved collection, consent, security, retention, access and communication processes. No healthcare privacy compliance certification is claimed for this concept.
