Care team

Who’s in the room.

Here’s who you might meet, and what you can ask each of them. A real practice would introduce its clinicians by name, with their qualifications.

Five roles, one conversation.

  • 1 · The first chair

    You

    You set the questions. You can ask for a pause, an explanation or a different approach at any point.

    Ask thisWhat would make this easier for me to say?

  • 2 · If you would like

    Someone you bring

    A partner, friend, family member or support worker. Ask how a support person can take part.

    Ask thisCan my support person stay for the whole conversation?

  • Illustrative model portrait

    3 · Gynecology, pregnancy or menopause

    A clinician from the right care team

    An authorized clinician owns assessment, options, consent and follow-up decisions for your care.

    Ask thisWho will I see, what are their qualifications, and who provides follow-up?

  • 4 · On request

    A chaperone

    Someone present during an examination for your comfort. Ask how to request one before the visit.

    Ask thisHow do I ask for a chaperone before the visit?

  • Illustrative model portrait

    5 · Before and after the room

    The patient care coordinator

    Routes your request, sorts out practical details and tells you who handles the next step. Clinical questions go to your clinician.

    Ask thisHow will I know the next step has been arranged, and who do I contact if it has not?

The teams behind the card

  • Gynecology care team

    A conversation about reproductive health, prevention or a concern you want taken seriously.

    Ask before you chooseWho will I see, and who provides follow-up?

  • Pregnancy care team

    Explore the questions involved in starting or transferring pregnancy care.

    Ask before you chooseHow does this team work with my other care providers?

  • Patient care coordinator

    Routes your request, sorts out practical details and tells you who handles the next step. Clinical questions go to your clinician.

    Ask before you chooseHow will I know the next step has been arranged?

02

For the clinic · note 2These are the three teams the coordinator routes to. They match the topic groups on the card.

Room to say what matters.

  1. Tell the team what would help: access, interpretation, a support person or an explanation before each step.

  2. Ask for a clear explanation of options, consent, costs and what needs deciding now or later.

  3. Ask who handles a concern about your care, communication or a bill.
