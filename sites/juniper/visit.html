What happens to your card

Who reads your card.

  1. 1

    The patient care coordinator reads it first.

    They confirm the kind of visit and the setting, and check what can be arranged.

  2. 2

    It goes to the right care team.

    Your topics decide who you would see.

    What would help is arranged before the day.
