Referring from a clinic? ↗
Will this create an appointment?+

No. The sample acknowledgement represents an item for intake review. It does not reserve a time, check benefits or approve care.

Can I enter my child’s details?+

The demo uses fixed fictional names and contact information. There are no fields for real patient information and no document uploads.

What would happen in a real clinic?+

A confirmed intake owner would review the inquiry, identify any missing information and explain the next step through the clinic’s approved systems.
