Family resources / Understanding the process

Benefits, approvals and the questions in between.

A guide to the conversation, not a coverage determination.

01

Which plan and service are we discussing?

Ask the billing team to confirm the specific plan and proposed service. A familiar insurer name alone does not establish participation, coverage or what your family may owe.

02

What needs approval before an assessment?

Some services may need authorization or supporting information. Ask which requirements apply, who requests approval and what happens if more information is needed.

03

What can we learn about possible costs?

Ask about estimates, deductibles, copayments and coinsurance, and how changes would be communicated. Keep a record of the date and source of the information you receive.

04

What if something changes?

New coverage, a different provider or a change in service can require review. Let the responsible team know through its approved communication route.

Put it into practice

Keep your questions somewhere easy to find. At the end of the conversation, confirm the next action and who will contact you.
