A small glossary

Translate the terms
before comparing numbers.

Deductible+

An amount you may need to pay for covered services before the plan begins paying for those services. Some benefits work differently, so ask what applies to this service and how much of the deductible remains.

Copay+

A fixed amount you pay for a covered service when your plan applies a copay. Ask whether it applies per visit or in another way for the proposed care.

Coinsurance+

A percentage of the covered service cost that you pay when your plan applies coinsurance. Ask which allowed amount the percentage is based on and whether a deductible applies first.

In network+

The provider has an agreement with the plan for the relevant care. Confirm the specific plan, provider, service and location; do not infer participation from an insurer logo.

Prior authorization+

A plan-specific approval process that may be required before a service. Ask what is approved, for which dates or limits, and what still needs checking. It is separate from a final payment decision.

Estimate, insurer statement and bill+

An estimate is a prediction based on stated assumptions. An insurer’s explanation of benefits describes how it processed a claim; it is not itself a provider bill. Ask billing to explain any difference before assuming the figures mean the same thing.

Term definitions draw on HealthCare.gov’s cost-sharing glossary. See also CMS: reading an explanation of benefits. Your plan determines the applicable details.

If the answer is not straightforward

Ask what is pending,
and what your options are.

Does Kitewell accept my plan?+

Kitewell is fictional and has no insurer contracts. A real clinic must verify its actual participation and explain how it checks an individual plan. This website cannot determine coverage.

What if the plan does not cover a proposed service?+

Ask for the reason, the relevant plan information and any review or appeal options that apply. Discuss appropriate care alternatives with the clinician, and ask billing to explain any financial options the actual clinic offers. None are assumed here.

What if our insurance changes?+

Contact the responsible team through its approved route. A change may require new benefits or authorization work. Do not assume an earlier review applies to the new plan.

What if a bill is different from the estimate?+

Ask billing to compare the estimate, the service delivered and the insurer’s response. Confirm what changed, what the family is being asked to pay and whom to contact if the difference remains unresolved.
