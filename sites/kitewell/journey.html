01 / The approach

Design the handoff
as carefully as the ad.

The question behind this concept: could clearer intake and follow-up help more inquiries reach an appropriate first appointment? That needs testing with a real clinic.

01

Let capacity shape acquisition.

A campaign should reflect where the clinic can assess and support new families. Availability changes the message and the next step.

Try three capacity scenarios ↗
02

Give each handoff an owner.

An inquiry needs a destination, an acknowledgement and someone responsible when it stalls. A form submission alone cannot show that.

Explore the operational journey ↗
03

Measure what happens next.

Completed intake and assessment cohorts tell a fuller story than form fills. Billing outcomes provide context with clear attribution limits.

See the measurement plan ↗
What informed the concept Published context, not project findings

A Michigan caregiver survey conducted in 2021 found that 35 of 48 respondents who pursued ABA reported a waitlist. Its small, regional sample supports investigating access barriers; it cannot estimate this fictional clinic’s wait time. Read the original report ↗

In an Autism Parenting Magazine subscriber survey, 71.7% of respondents using ABA said a doctor recommended it. The article does not report the responding sample size. This suggests a referral route worth testing, not a forecast of referral volume. Read the survey article ↗

To validate the hypothesis: review inquiry cohorts, response timestamps and missing-information reasons with clinic staff, then test the proposed changes. No clinic interviews or operational audit were conducted for this concept.

05 / What success would mean

Follow the cohort,
not just the click.

A proposed measurement plan, with targets set after a baseline. No patient outcomes, conversion gains or revenue impact have been measured.

01

Completed intake

Intake lead

DefinitionUnique completed intakes divided by unique intake starts in the same cohort, with enough follow-up time to finish.

Source and windowReviewed counts from the intake system. Weekly operational review, monthly cohort reporting.

Question it answersAre families able to complete the next step?

02

Response and unresolved items

Intake + billing

DefinitionMedian business hours to first contact attempt, share still uncontacted, missing-item rate and time to resolution.

Source and windowIntake timestamps and a defined requirements checklist. Weekly queue review.

Question it answersWhere is the queue stalling, and who can act?

03

Completed assessments

Clinical + marketing

DefinitionCompleted assessments divided by completed intakes, grouped by inquiry month. Channel cost uses spend and completed assessments from the same acquisition cohort.

Source and windowApproved scheduling and source totals. A proposed 90-day window, adjusted after reviewing actual wait times. Self-reported source is directional attribution.

Question it answersWhich inquiries progress to an assessment within the observation window?

04

Claim quality and collections context

Billing partner

DefinitionFirst-pass acceptance uses unique original claims. Registration/eligibility denial rates use adjudicated claims with agreed reason categories. Open claims are reported separately.

Source and windowClearinghouse and billing reports. Fixed mature cohorts; a proposed 180-day view is a starting assumption, not a universal rule.

Question it answersAre intake-related issues changing? Compare payer mix, staffing and service mix before attributing a change. Collections are context, not proven marketing ROI.

An eligibility response is not authorization or a payment guarantee. Clearinghouse acceptance, payer acknowledgement, adjudication and payment reconciliation remain distinct. CMS guidance ↗

Sources and scope notes
