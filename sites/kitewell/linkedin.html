KitewellSample brand post · Referral process guide

A referral needs an acknowledgement that distinguishes receipt from readiness. The proposed Kitewell handoff identifies an intake owner, any outstanding items and the next action. Clinical suitability, plan requirements and availability are reviewed separately. Share records only through a verified clinic channel with the appropriate authority.

KitewellSample brand post · Role guide

The person providing a session may have a different role from the clinician responsible for the plan. A BCBA can assess, plan and supervise behavior-analytic services. An RBT works under supervision rather than independently. Ask for actual names, credentials, supervision arrangements and a contact for concerns.

KitewellSample brand post · Cost-preparation guide

An insurer’s name on a website is not an individual coverage decision. Before agreeing to care, request an explanation tied to the exact service, provider, location and plan. Ask about cost sharing, approval limits and charges outside covered care. Keep the estimate and the date of the review.
