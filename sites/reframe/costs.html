Costs

Who sends the bill?

In orthopedics, one visit can lead to bills from several places. Ask about each one before it happens.

1

Clinic owner note: Orthopedic bills surprise people because they come from several places. This page is the conversation your billing team has after the statement arrives, moved to before the visit.

  1. First specialist visit

    Who usually billsUsually the practice

    AskIs this a specialist visit under my plan, and does my plan need a referral first?

  2. X-ray or MRI

    Who usually billsThe practice, or a separate imaging center

    AskIs imaging (X-ray, MRI) billed by you or by a separate imaging center?

  3. Brace, boot or crutches

    Who usually billsSometimes a separate equipment supplier

    AskIs a brace or other equipment billed separately?

  4. Physical therapy

    Who usually billsPer session, over a series

    AskHow many therapy visits does my plan cover, and is there a copay each time?

  5. Surgery

    Who usually billsOften three bills: surgeon, facility, anesthesia

    AskIf surgery is planned, will the surgeon, facility and anesthesia bill separately?

2

Clinic owner note: Each card is one question a patient can ask the front desk before a visit: imaging, equipment, therapy visit limits and the three parts of a surgery bill.

Therapy runs over several sessions, so ask about the whole plan, not one visit. A website can’t tell you how many sessions you’ll need.

Questions for the front desk

  1. Is this a specialist visit under my plan, and does my plan need a referral first?
  2. Is imaging (X-ray, MRI) billed by you or by a separate imaging center?
  3. Is a brace or other equipment billed separately?
  4. How many therapy visits does my plan cover, and is there a copay each time?
  5. If surgery is planned, will the surgeon, facility and anesthesia bill separately?
  6. Who do I call with a billing question afterwards?
