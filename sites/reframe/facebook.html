Do I need a referral for physical therapy?

We hear this more than any other question. The honest answer is: it depends on two things.

  1. The rules where you live.
  2. Your plan, which may want a referral even when the law doesn’t.

Check both before you book. Our article walks through each, and the referral guide covers what to have ready.
