First-visit planner · 11 items

What do you want to get back to?

Turn “it hurts” into a useful conversation about your day.

1

Clinic owner note: This checklist is the offer behind the social posts. No email gate: the download is made in the browser, and the patient arrives with the details intake would otherwise ask for.

1The activity you want back

  • Name one activity that has become difficult, and how often you do it.
  • Say what stops you: pain, stiffness, weakness, giving way, or not trusting it.
  • Note what you have changed already, like shorter walks or taking the lift.
  • Pick the goal that matters most for the next few months.

2How it started

  • When it started, and whether there was a moment it changed.
  • What makes it easier and what makes it harder.
  • Earlier care for the same area: therapy, injections, a brace or surgery.
  • Imaging you have had, where it was done, and whether you can get the report.

3Questions for the specialist

  • What is each proposed next step meant to answer?
  • Is therapy, imaging or a follow-up next, and why?
  • What should I keep doing, or avoid, until then?

What to say when you call

“I’d like a first orthopedic appointment for my knee. It started in March. I don’t have a referral, and my plan is through work.”
