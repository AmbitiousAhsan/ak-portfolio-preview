Rehab preparation pack · 11 items

Your first therapy visit, unpacked.

What to bring and which practical questions to ask before an assessment.

1

Clinic owner note: This checklist is the offer behind the social posts. No email gate: the download is made in the browser, and the patient arrives with the details intake would otherwise ask for.

1Before you book

  • Ask whether your plan needs a referral or authorization for therapy.
  • Ask how many therapy visits your plan covers in a year.
  • Pick the setting and the times that fit your week.
  • After surgery, ask when your surgeon wants therapy to start.

2Bring and wear

  • Your referral and any post-operative instructions.
  • Clothes you can move in, and shorts if it’s your knee or hip.
  • The shoes you walk or train in most.
  • A list of the movements and tasks that are hard right now.

3Ask at the first session

  • What are we working toward first, and how will we check it?
  • What does the home plan involve, and how long will it take each day?
  • When do you update my referring clinician?

What to say when you call

“I have a physical therapy referral for my shoulder. Can I book a first assessment, and do you need anything from my plan first?”
