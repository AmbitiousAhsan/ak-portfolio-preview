Clinic owner note: This page catches the “I just hurt it” search. Its job is to send emergencies to 911 and non-emergencies to the right visit before anyone drives over.

This is your route if…

  • A new, non-emergency injury from the last few days
  • A twist, fall or strain while you were active
  • You want to know where to go before you travel

Better route if…

  • It is an emergency: call 911Call 911

  • It has been going on for weeksOrthopedic consultation
  • You already have a therapy planPhysical therapy

Clinic owner note: The “better route” links catch misbooked visits before they happen: someone with a PT referral doesn’t book a specialist consult by mistake, and an emergency is sent to 911.

Wear and bring

Loose clothing that fits over a swollen joint, a splint or a boot.

  • What happened, when, and what you did straight after

The question people ask most

“Can every injury be seen here?”

No website should imply that. Confirm the practice’s scope before travel. For a medical emergency, use emergency services rather than this demo.

3

Clinic owner note: What to wear and bring is the most common pre-visit call for orthopedics and therapy. Answering it here, per route, is one less call and fewer first visits spent changing clothes.
