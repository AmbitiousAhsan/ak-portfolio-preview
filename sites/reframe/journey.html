What happens after you ask

Your request passes through a few hands before and after your first visit. Here they are, in order.

1

Clinic owner note: This is the operational model behind the site: social post to intake, care and billing. Real integration would need confirmed systems, data ownership, clinical and privacy review, field mapping, failure handling and audit trails. None of that is built here; this sample site does not connect to EMR, scheduling, eligibility, claims or payment systems.

  1. 1

    You ask

    Concern, activity goal and referral route

    You choose a care route and a place. The planner only carries the visit type, never symptoms.

  2. 2

    Route

    Coordinator checks referral and records requirements

    A coordinator checks the referral, if there is one, and which records are needed.

  3. 3

    Prepare

    Benefits and authorization questions resolved separately

    Plan benefits and any authorization are checked separately from booking.

  4. 4

    Care

    Specialist documents an agreed care plan

    The specialist agrees a plan with you and writes it down.

  5. 5

    Follow through

    Therapy team receives approved referral and instructions

    If therapy is next, the therapy team receives the referral and instructions.

  6. 6

    Close the loop

    Progress feedback, claim review and patient billing

    Progress goes back to the referring clinician, and billing follows the visits that were documented.

2

Clinic owner note: What the website itself demonstrates: body-area and situation routes from posts, checklists and the body map; a sample plan with place and question; an acknowledgement naming the next team; and cost, care and admin questions kept apart.

This sample site doesn’t connect to any booking, records or billing system.
