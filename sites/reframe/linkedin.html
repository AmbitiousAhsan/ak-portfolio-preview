Overview

Reframe is an orthopedic and physical therapy practice built so the patient never does the routing work. Specialist visits and therapy share a building at the Movement Center; Westside Studio is therapy only. One referral coordinator checks every referral, and therapy progress is reported back to the referring clinician when coordination is needed.

Why we ask patients what they want to get back to.

“Knee pain” describes a symptom. “The stairs at work” describes a goal you can plan around and review. Every first visit at Reframe starts with the activity, so the plan has something to measure itself against.

How a referral is handed off

  1. Concern, activity goal and referral route
  2. Coordinator checks referral and records requirements
  3. Benefits and authorization questions resolved separately
  4. Specialist documents an agreed care plan
  5. Therapy team receives approved referral and instructions
  6. Progress feedback, claim review and patient billing
