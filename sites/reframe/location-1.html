Clinic owner note: Parking, transit and step-free access are the location questions callers ask most. Here they are answered on the page, with sample values a real clinic would replace.

Before you set off

  • Confirm the real address and the entrance used for your visit.
  • Ask about step-free access, elevators and accessible parking.
  • Check that your service is offered at this place.
  • Ask what arrival time and documents the practice needs.

What it’s like

  • Specialist consults and the therapy floor share one building in this concept, so a plan agreed upstairs can start downstairs.
  • Ask which entrance your visit uses and where to check in.
  • Ask about step-free access, elevators and parking before you travel.
