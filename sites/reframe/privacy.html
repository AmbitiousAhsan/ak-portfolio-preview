Reframe/Demo privacy

Demo privacy

No patient information belongs in this demo.

Use the preset choices to explore. There are no health-history fields, uploads, payments or clinical messages.

Local interactions

The body map, the view switch and the visit planner use preset choices held in the current page’s memory; the view travels between pages in the link. Follow, save, filter and storyboard controls change only this preview. Refreshing clears them. A checklist download is created by your browser from the page itself.

No clinical submission

The planner does not submit a request, reserve an appointment, create a patient record or contact a practice. The Call button places no call. Do not rely on this site to obtain care.

Hosting and portfolio

The hosting platform may process ordinary request data to serve the site. This build contains no analytics tags and makes no third-party requests. See the portfolio privacy page for the wider website.

Real-world requirements

A real practice would need approved collection, consent, security, retention, access and communication processes. No healthcare privacy compliance certification is claimed for this concept.
