In the room

What the visit involves

  1. 1

    Options

    Discuss the proposed procedure, alternatives, risks and expected recovery with the surgeon.

  2. 2

    Logistics

    Confirm the facility, preparation instructions and separate cost components.

  3. 3

    Recovery

    Understand follow-up, rehabilitation and who manages questions after discharge.

Wear and bring

Bring a support person if you would like one in the conversation, and something to write the answers on.

  • Your questions about alternatives and recovery time
  • Who can drive you home and help in the first weeks
  • Any preparation instructions from the surgeon

The question people ask most

“Is a consultation a commitment to surgery?”

A consultation is a place to understand the options and ask questions. A treatment decision requires an individual clinical discussion and informed consent.

What usually happens

Where this sits in a recovery

In order. Not a promise or a timetable: your own sequence is set with your care team, and it can pause, repeat or loop back.

4

Clinic owner note: The “you are here” marker tells a patient what comes after this visit. For the clinic it sets up the check-in, the point in a plan where patients most often drop off.

  1. 1

    Consult

    Someone listens, examines and explains the options before anything is decided.

    Usually owned by Orthopedic care team

    Ask What is each proposed next step meant to answer?

  2. 2

    Plan You are here

    You agree what you are working toward, what happens first and how progress will be reviewed.

    Usually owned by Your clinician, with you

    Ask How will we know whether the plan needs to change?

  3. 3

    Therapy

    Sessions and a home plan built around your goals, adjusted as you go.

    Usually owned by Physical therapy team

    Ask How often, for how long, and what does my plan cover?

  4. 4

    Check-in

    Progress is reviewed, and the referring team is looped in when coordination is needed. Sometimes that sends you back a step.

    Usually owned by Therapist and referring clinician

    Ask Who do I contact if something changes between visits?

A check-in can send you back to the plan. That is part of the sequence, not a setback.
