Your goal, in one sentence

“I’d like to ”

For example: “…look less tired in photos”, “…even out patches of colour”, “…understand my options before next year”.

You are researching an optional treatment and want to discuss suitability, expectations and cost. Saying the goal in your own words keeps the conversation about you, not a treatment menu.

Cosmetic

A cosmetic goal

What the visit involves
A consultation, not a treatment. You describe what you hope to change; the clinician talks through suitability, options, risks, recovery and realistic limits. Deciding is a separate step, taken in your own time.
Insurance usually covers
Very little. Treatments chosen mainly for appearance are elective, and most health plans exclude them. A few overlap with medical care in specific situations; only your plan can say whether yours does.
Usually not covered
Treatments done mainly to change appearance, and their follow-ups. Ask the practice for a written estimate before you decide; if you are paying yourself, you can ask for a good faith estimate.
