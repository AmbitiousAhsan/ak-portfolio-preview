Which appointment do I ask for?

It’s the first question on our phone line, and the answer is simpler than it sounds.

  1. Something new, changing or uncomfortable: a medical visit.
  2. Nothing worries you and you’d like to change how something looks: a cosmetic consultation.
  3. Not sure: start with a medical visit.

The two are booked separately and never share a waiting list. The pocket list below gives you the sentence to say when you call.
