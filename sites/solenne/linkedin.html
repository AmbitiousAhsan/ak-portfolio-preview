Overview

Solenne is a dermatology practice built around two doors. Medical requests go to medical scheduling and a dermatology team; elective requests go to a consultation coordinator. Neither queue inherits the other’s backlog, every pathology result has one named owner, and every elective plan is given in writing before treatment.

Why our medical pages never mention cosmetics.

Patients tell dermatology practices the same worry: that a medical visit will become a pitch. The fix isn’t a promise in small print; it’s structure. Separate pages, separate booking, separate posts, and a rule that the medical path never links to elective care.

See the two doorsThe operating model

Two queues, one practice

  1. Medical concern or cosmetic goal
  2. Separate clinical and elective scheduling queues
  3. Coverage questions or written self-pay estimate
  4. Clinician assessment and informed decision
  5. Approved care plan, follow-up and any results
  6. Medical claim review or agreed elective payment
